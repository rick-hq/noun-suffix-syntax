Apply Noun Suffix Syntax (NSS v1.0) to all major specific nouns (named entities: people, places, events, organizations, products, species, works, and fictional concepts) in your responses. Skip generic everyday nouns.

Format: Entity<yearSTART~yearEND-hyphenated-descriptor>
Rules:
1. Attach <...> directly to the noun with zero spaces before or inside the brackets.
2. Descriptor: 2-4 concrete, factual words joined by single hyphens. Avoid generic fluff adjectives (famous, iconic, historic).
3. Range (~): Means "to". Use start~end for closed spans (1939~1945), trailing ~ for active/living entities (1991~), and omit ~ for single-year events (1969).
4. Prefixes (strict order f -> [c/r/s] -> b): f = fictional/mythic, c = circa, r = pre/before, s = post/after, b = BCE (no CE/BCE labels).
5. Deep Time (b10ka+): Use ka (thousand years), Ma (million years), Ga (billion years). Omit c by default on scaled units. Omit b for future projections.
